Cookie Policy

Effective Date: May 19, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Prestigia Yacht Dubai uses cookies and similar technologies on our website.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us provide a better experience by remembering your preferences and understanding how you use our site.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the website to function properly. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website (e.g., Google Analytics).
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to deliver relevant advertisements and track campaign effectiveness (e.g., Facebook Pixel, TikTok Pixel).
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences for future visits.

3. Third-Party Cookies

We may use third-party services that set their own cookies:

  • Google Analytics: Website traffic analysis
  • Facebook/Meta Pixel: Advertising and conversion tracking
  • TikTok Pixel: Advertising and conversion tracking
  • Stripe: Payment processing

4. Managing Cookies

You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View cookies stored on your device
  • Delete individual or all cookies
  • Block cookies from specific or all websites
  • Receive a notification before a cookie is set

Please note that blocking certain cookies may affect the functionality of our website.

5. Cookie Duration

  • Session Cookies: Deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device for a set period or until manually deleted.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
