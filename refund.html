Refund Policy

Effective Date: May 19, 2026

At Prestigia Yacht Dubai, we strive to provide exceptional service. If you need to cancel or modify your booking, please review our refund policy below.

1. Cancellation by Guest

  • 48+ hours before charter: Full refund (100%) will be issued to the original payment method.
  • 24-48 hours before charter: 50% refund will be issued.
  • Less than 24 hours before charter: No refund will be provided.

2. Cancellation by Prestigia Yacht

  • If we cancel your charter due to weather, mechanical issues, or safety concerns, you will receive a full refund (100%) or the option to reschedule at no additional cost.
  • We will notify you as soon as possible if a cancellation is necessary.

3. Payment Method Refunds

  • Credit/Debit Card: Refunds are processed within 5-10 business days and will appear on your original payment method.
  • Cryptocurrency (USDT TRC20): Refunds are processed in USDT to the same wallet address used for payment. Processing time is 24-48 hours.

4. No-Show Policy

If you do not arrive at the agreed pickup location or marina within 30 minutes of your scheduled departure time, the charter will be considered a no-show and no refund will be issued.

5. Early Termination

If you choose to end your charter early for any reason, no partial refund will be provided. The full charter fee applies regardless of actual time spent on board.

6. Weather-Related Changes

  • In case of adverse weather conditions, the captain may alter the route, delay departure, or cancel the charter for safety reasons.
  • If the charter is delayed, we will offer an alternative time or full refund.
  • If the route is modified due to weather, no partial refund is provided as the charter still takes place.

7. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us with your booking ID:

  • Email: info@prestigiayacht.ae
  • Phone: +971 XX XXX XXXX
  • Response Time: Within 24 hours

8. Disputes

If you have a dispute regarding a refund, please contact us first. We will make every effort to resolve the issue promptly and fairly. If unresolved, disputes are subject to the laws of the United Arab Emirates.
